# Concepts

Concept pages contain distilled, reusable knowledge. The existing
`platforms/`, `business/` and `expertise/` directories remain canonical for
platform and operating playbooks. Cross-platform technical subjects such as
programmatic advertising may live under `concepts/<domain>/` so related
concepts stay grouped; `wiki_type: concept` makes the role explicit.

Programmatic advertising concepts are indexed from `concepts/programmatic/`
and categorized as `programmatic_advertising`.
